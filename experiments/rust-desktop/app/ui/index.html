    Terms & Privacy

    Pear Desktop Rust Preview uses YouTube API Services. By using this preview, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

    On this device

    Pear stores your queue, selected video, known titles, playlists, playback preference, and agreement in its local profile. You can remove queue entries and playlists in Pear. To erase the profile, close Pear and delete its profile directory.

    YouTube and Google

    When you select or restore a video, Pear shares its ID with YouTube to load the embedded player and fetch details and artwork. Details use YouTube oEmbed or the YouTube Data API with a key you configure. When you open a public playlist in Pear, Pear sends its playlist ID to the embedded player for playback or to the YouTube Data API when you ask to browse it. The playback session is temporary. Pear doesn’t access your private YouTube account or save the playlist’s playback items to your local queue. Data API search and browsing use the configured key’s project quota. When you choose Open in browser for a YouTube Music search, Pear passes your search text to your browser to submit to YouTube Music.

    Pear uses separate private WebViews for the controls and player. YouTube and Google may receive your IP address and playback activity, use cookies or similar technologies, and serve videos, artwork, or ads. Links you open in your browser use that browser’s session.

    Lyrics

    When you choose Find lyrics, Pear sends the track title, artist, and duration when available to LRCLIB, which also receives your IP address. Pear keeps lyrics in memory and clears them when you change videos or quit.
